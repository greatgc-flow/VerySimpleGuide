# Test-Driven Evidence and Traceability

For each scoped requirement record ID, applicability, authority/boundary, acceptance rule, suitable falsification method, implementation reference, evidence/gate reference and disposition.

A change may use RED regression, contract tests, model checking, proof, simulation or a bounded research spike according to risk. Where reproducing an existing defect is required, provide RED evidence or a documented approved exception. Do not measure quality by the count of tests alone: catalog obligation IDs and actual test instances are different metrics.

**Target architecture conformance ≠ current product assurance.** Verify each on its own evidence axis.
