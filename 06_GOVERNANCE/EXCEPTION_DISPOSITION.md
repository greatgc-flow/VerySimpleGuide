# Exception and Disposition

Do not silently bury gaps or invent compliance. Record exception ID, impacted scope, reason, owner, acceptance authority, residual risk, expiry or revisit trigger, alternative safeguard and evidence link where relevant.

Useful outcomes include `ACCEPT`, `REJECT`, `DEFER`, `DUPLICATE`, `N_A`, `PASS` and `REOPEN`. Closing an accepted improvement requires the exact release and independently observed post-outcome, or an explicit approved exception. Preserve historical decisions and later reversal events.
