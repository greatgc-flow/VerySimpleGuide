# Implementation Waves (Advisory)

A commonly useful sequence—not a mandated delivery plan—is:

- **Wave 0:** boundaries, contracts, schemas and regression/counterexample fixtures.
- **Wave 1:** minimal authoritative capability.
- **Wave 2:** query projections and read paths.
- **Wave 3:** diagnostics, measurements and evidence.
- **Wave 4:** recovery and data safety.
- **Wave 5:** external adapters and providers.
- **Wave 6:** bounded orchestration, federation and public protocols.
- **Wave 7:** optimization and ecosystem extensions.

Adapt this sequence to risks, learning goals and dependencies. A bounded interoperability spike may precede the internal authority implementation, but prototypes must not silently become production source-of-truth systems.
