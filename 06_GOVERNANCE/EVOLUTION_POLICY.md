# Controlled Standard Evolution

Apply the [versioned evolution contract](../02_STANDARD/UNIFIED_STANDARD.md). Before adding a category, challenge the existing mapping against the workload; prefer reuse where it preserves ownership. A justified PRODUCT_SPECIFIC or ADAPT approach is not inferior conformance. Project version pins remain upgradeable.

The package's own [work trace](../04_ASSURANCE/package-trace.json) is validated by CI with the product trace contract; examples remain illustrative. Local implementation/proof does not complete adoption: retain IN_PROGRESS until an actual commit/PR and CI deployment reference, measured outcome and closure/revisit evidence exist. Prior records can be supplied with `--prior-project-trace`; do not invent operational success to close the record.
