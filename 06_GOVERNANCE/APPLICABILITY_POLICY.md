# Applicability Policy

Every obligation should receive an explicit scoped assessment: `REQUIRED`, `OPTIONAL`, `N_A`, `PASS` or `DEFERRED`.

Do not hide missing ownership behind "where applicable", "where practical" or "when feasible" without recording a decision. `N_A`, `PASS` and `DEFERRED` require reasons; deferrals require owner and revisit trigger, while passing decisions require relevant evidence and candidate identity.
